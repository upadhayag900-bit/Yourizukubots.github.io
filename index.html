Section 01

About Isukobit

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit ek Telegram-based File Store Bot hai jo users ko files upload, search, download aur manage karne ki suvidha deta hai. Hamara bot Telegram ke official Bot API platform par kaam karta hai aur user-generated content ko store aur share karne ki facility deta hai.

Isukobit koi bhi file khud host nahi karta. Saari files Telegram ke secure servers par store hoti hain aur bot sirf unhe reference karta hai. Hum ek middleware service hain jo aapko files access karne mein madad karta hai.

Copyright Policy

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit copyright laws ka poora samman karta hai. Agar aap koi copyrighted content upload karte hain toh aap khud uske liye responsible hain.

7.1 Aapki Responsibility

Sirf wahi content upload karein jiska aapko:

  • Owner hain, YA
  • Legal permission hai, YA
  • Fair use ke under aata hai, YA
  • Public domain mein hai

7.2 Copyright Infringement Report

Agar aapko lagta hai ki aapka copyrighted work Isukobit par unauthorized tarike se upload hua hai, toh aap DMCA takedown notice bhej sakte hain.

7.3 DMCA Notice Requirements

Aapka notice is tarah hona chahiye:

  1. Aapki physical ya electronic signature
  2. Copyrighted work ka description
  3. Infringing material ka location (URL ya file code)
  4. Aapka contact info (email, phone)
  5. Good faith statement
  6. Perjury statement

Complete DMCA notice ke liye Section 11 dekhein.

7.4 Counter-Notice

Agar aapko lagta hai ki aapka content galat tarike se remove hua hai, toh aap counter-notice bhej sakte hain.

DMCA & Takedown Policy

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) comply karta hai. Agar aapko copyright infringement dikhti hai, toh aap takedown notice bhej sakte hain.

11.1 DMCA Notice Format

Apna notice @IsukobitSupport par bhejein:

📝
Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice
To: @IsukobitSupport
Include: Neeche di gayi saari details

11.2 Notice Contents

  1. Identification of Work: Aapka copyrighted work kya hai (title, description, registration)
  2. Infringing Material: Isukobit par kahan hai (file code, URL)
  3. Your Contact Info: Naam, email, phone, address
  4. Good Faith Statement: "I believe in good faith that the use is not authorized..."
  5. Accuracy Statement: "Under penalty of perjury, the information is accurate..."
  6. Signature: Physical ya electronic
