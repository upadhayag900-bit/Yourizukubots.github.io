Section 01

About Isukobit

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit is a Telegram-based File Store Bot that allows users to upload, search, download, and manage files. Our bot operates on Telegram's official Bot API platform and provides facilities to store and share user-generated content.

Isukobit is a passive middleware service. We do not host, create, or verify any files ourselves. All files are stored on Telegram's secure servers, and the bot only references them. We are an intermediary platform, just like Telegram itself.

Section 07

Copyright Policy

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit is a passive intermediary platform — just like Telegram and Google Drive. We do not pre-screen or verify content uploaded by users.

7.1 Intermediary Status

Isukobit is an "intermediary" under the Indian IT Act, 2000 (Section 79) and DMCA Section 512. This means:

  • We host content but do not create it
  • We are not responsible for the legality of content
  • Copyright liability for user-uploaded content rests with the user
  • We follow a DMCA-compliant takedown process

7.2 Copyright Takedown Policy

Copyrighted content on Isukobit is not automatically removed. Content is only removed when:

  • An official DMCA notice arrives from Telegram
  • A copyright holder sends a legal takedown request
  • A court order is issued
  • Content is clearly CSAM or otherwise illegal

7.3 Your Responsibility

You are fully responsible for any content you upload. Only upload content that:

  • You own, OR
  • You have legal permission for, OR
  • Falls under fair use, OR
  • Is in the public domain

7.4 Repeat Infringer Policy

If a user receives 3 or more valid DMCA complaints, their account may be permanently banned.

📋
For Copyright Holders: If you find your copyrighted work on Isukobit, send us a DMCA notice. We will take action on valid notices within 48-72 hours. Details are in Section 11.

Section 11

DMCA & Takedown Policy

Last updated: 15 Feb 2026

Isukobit complies with the DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) and the Indian IT Act. Proper takedown process must be followed to remove content.

11.1 When Content Will Be Removed

Content on Isukobit is not automatically removed. Content will only be removed when:

  1. An official DMCA notice arrives from Telegram
  2. A valid legal notice arrives from the copyright owner
  3. A court order is issued
  4. CSAM or clearly illegal content is detected
  5. There is a direct threat to safety

11.2 DMCA Notice Format

Send your notice to @IsukobitSupport:

📝
Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice
To: @IsukobitSupport
Include: All details listed below

11.3 Notice Contents (Required)

  1. Identification of Work: What is your copyrighted work (title, description, registration number)
  2. Infringing Material: Where is it on Isukobit (file code, URL, exact location)
  3. Your Contact Info: Name, email, phone, address
  4. Good Faith Statement: "I believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner..."
  5. Accuracy Statement: "Under penalty of perjury, the information in this notice is accurate..."
  6. Signature: Physical or electronic signature

11.4 Action Timeline

Acknowledgment
24-48 hours
Review
3-7 days
Action
7-14 days

11.5 Counter-Notice

If your content was removed incorrectly, you may send a counter-notice. It must include the same details plus a declaration that the removal was a mistake.

11.6 Repeat Infringer Policy

After 3 valid DMCA complaints, a user's account will be permanently banned.

⚠️
Important: No content will be automatically removed without a copyright holder. Send a proper legal notice first, then we will act.
